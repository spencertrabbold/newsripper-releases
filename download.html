News.Ripper 3.9.4

Rumble support update for limited newsroom testing.

Download NewsRipper 3.9.4 Installer

What changed

  • Added support for public Rumble videos when normal yt-dlp extraction is blocked, using the official Rumble embed and browser-assisted media discovery.
  • Selects the highest available safe HLS rendition and produces a Premiere-friendly H.264/AAC MP4.
  • Preserves existing URL safety, filename hardening, magic-byte validation, Zone.Identifier, cancellation, cleanup, and finalization.
  • A refreshed, calm, ocean-inspired design built for the newsroom, with the paste-and-download flow front and center.
  • Every download now shows its stage at a glance: Downloading, Merging audio & video, Converting to broadcast MP4, and Ready.
  • Settings moved into a slide-over panel that stays out of your way.
  • New app icon and launch screen.
  • Includes everything from 3.8.x: hardware-accelerated conversions, parallel downloads, the self-updating downloader engine, YouTube live-stream (VOD) support, plain-English errors, and disk-space and cookie-file safeguards.
Note: This page is for limited newsroom testing. Code signing is pending.
